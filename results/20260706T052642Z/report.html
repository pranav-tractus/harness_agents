Does the automatic cleanup step help?

After a model reads a document, our system runs an automatic cleanup step — fixing units, recalculating totals, double-checking against the source chat. This shows how much that step improves each model's raw answer.

ModelBefore cleanupAfter cleanupImprovement
GPT-5.581.0%86.0%+5.0 pts
Claude Fable 585.0%88.7%+3.7 pts
Claude Sonnet 586.0%89.6%+3.6 pts
Gemini 2.5 Pro84.1%85.9%+1.9 pts
Claude Sonnet 4.688.3%89.8%+1.5 pts

Across all models, cleanup added an average of +3.1 percentage points — taking overall accuracy from 84.9% (raw model output) to 88.0% (final, cleaned-up output). Both numbers are well above the 80.2% we'd get from simple rule-based extraction with no AI model at all.

What this means

01
For most teams: use Claude Sonnet 4.6
It's the single most accurate model (89.8%) and the second-fastest (12.7s), making it the safest default choice.
02
If speed matters most: use Claude Sonnet 5
It's barely behind on accuracy (89.6%, a 0.2-point gap) but is the fastest model tested, finishing in 11.2 seconds.
03
Avoid Gemini 2.5 Pro for this task
It's both the least accurate (85.9%) and by far the slowest (33.7s — 3× the fastest) — there's no scenario in this test where it comes out ahead.
